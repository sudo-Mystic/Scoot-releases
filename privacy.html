Scoot.

Privacy

Last updated: 27 September 2026

Short version: Scoot keeps your data on your phone. There is no account, no ads, and nothing is sold.

Your scooter and your phone

Scoot talks to your own scooter over Bluetooth. Ride data, cluster alerts, and navigation move between your phone and your scooter only.

Stored on your device

Your trip history and your last parked location are saved on your phone, not on any server. Uninstalling the app removes them.

Location permission

Scoot asks for location access for two features: remembering where you parked, and navigation. Location is used on the device for these features and is not sent anywhere.

Analytics

Analytics are off by default. If you turn on "Share usage stats" under Settings, the app sends anonymous usage statistics through PostHog to help improve Scoot. No personal identifiers, no trip routes, no location trails.

Nothing sold, no accounts

Scoot has no accounts and no advertising. Your data is never sold or shared with third parties.
