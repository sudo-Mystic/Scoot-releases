Install

  1. Download the APK above on your phone.
  2. Open the file. Android will ask you to allow installs from your browser or file manager — allow it once.
  3. Tap Install.
  4. After installing, updates show up inside the app under Settings → App.

Verify your download

Pick the APK you downloaded and this page checks its SHA-256 against the release manifest. Everything happens in your browser — the file never leaves your phone.
